About the Program

A community outreach initiative to raise awareness about human trafficking, its warning signs, and how students, educators, and the public can help prevent it and support survivors.

Why Attend?

Awareness is the first line of defense against trafficking. This program equips you with the knowledge and resources to protect yourself, your peers, and your community.

Our Mission

This initiative is organized to educate students, faculty, and the wider community about the realities of human trafficking, dispel common myths, and build a network of informed, empowered individuals ready to act.

Through partnerships with NGOs, law enforcement, and survivor advocacy groups, we aim to create a sustained impact — turning awareness into action.
